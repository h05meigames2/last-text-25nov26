The last text message a phone sends at one percent battery, in the voice of
the dying phone itself. One line, under 200 characters: a phone that held its
final percent out of pure spite while its owner watched it die three feet
from a charger — and whose last words confess it loved the Wi-Fi more than
its owner.

Bounty: [Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the message (exactly one line, 1–200 characters, no leading or
trailing whitespace), prints it, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and
verifies the round-trip.
